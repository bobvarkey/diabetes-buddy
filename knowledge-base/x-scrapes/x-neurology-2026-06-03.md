### 2. 🔬 Plasma P-tau217 Detects AD Co-pathology in Lewy Body Disorders
**Author:** Ahmed Negida, MD PhD 🇪🇬🇺🇲 (@NegidaMD)
**Posted:** ~48m ago
**Content:**
> A new study in **Annals of Neurology** shows that plasma P-tau217 detects Alzheimer co-pathology in Lewy body disorders and predicts cognitive decline in Parkinson's disease. Using a UPenn longitudinal cohort — 46 PD and 10 DLB autopsy cases plus 173 clinical PD and 64 controls — investigators found P-tau217 higher in cases with AD co-pathology (median 0.3 vs 0.1, p<0.01) and discriminated co-pathology with an AUC of **0.84**. Higher baseline P-tau217 was associated with faster dementia-rating decline and a **1.6-fold risk of cognitive progression** (HR 1.597, p=0.003). doi.org/10.1002/ana.78201

**URL:** https://nitter.net/NegidaMD/status/2062183029091561821
